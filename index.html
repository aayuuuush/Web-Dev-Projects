Our Biomedical Equipment

Discover our range of high-quality, reliable medical equipment designed to meet the needs of modern healthcare facilities

Digital ECG Machine
Diagnostic
Advanced 12-lead ECG system with wireless connectivity, touch screen interface, and automated analysis. Features include high-resolution display, internal memory for 500+ ECGs, and USB/Bluetooth data transfer.
Patient Monitor
Monitoring
Multi-parameter patient monitor with 15" touchscreen, continuous monitoring of ECG, SpO2, NIBP, temperature, and respiration. Features modular design, long battery life, and central monitoring compatibility.
Infusion Pump
Therapy
Smart infusion pump with drug library, dose error reduction system, and wireless connectivity. Supports multiple infusion modes with precision accuracy of ±2%. Equipped with comprehensive safety alarms.
Ventilator System
Critical Care
ICU ventilator with advanced pulmonary diagnostics, multiple ventilation modes, and intuitive touch interface. Features include built-in oxygen blender, internal battery, and comprehensive monitoring parameters.
Ultrasound System
Imaging
Portable ultrasound system with 15" HD display, multi-frequency probes, and advanced imaging modes. Features include elastography, 3D/4D imaging, wireless connectivity, and extended battery life.
Defibrillator
Emergency
Biphasic defibrillator with AED and manual modes, CPR feedback system, and 8-hour battery life. Features clear voice prompts, ECG monitoring, and data recording for post-event analysis.
